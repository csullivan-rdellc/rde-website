THE PROBLEM

Unmonitored AI Creates Critical Operational and Contractual Liabilities

Defense suppliers and commercial teams face severe operational exposure when adopting AI without oversight.

01

Shadow AI and Data Loss

Employees paste proprietary code, operational plans, and Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) into third-party AI models without IT authorization.

02

Direct Prompt Injections

Adversarial inputs bypass internal controls, manipulating model logic and exposing sensitive data pipelines.

03

Audit Non-Compliance

Federal contracting mandates require strict adherence to NIST and CMMC standards. Unaudited AI systems jeopardize your contract awards and certifications.

THE SOLUTION

RDE AI Guard: Enterprise AI Defense Without the Dedicated Team Overhead

RDE AI Guard combines proven open-source inspection tools with hands-on compliance engineering. Our team hosts, monitors, and validates your AI infrastructure against rigorous federal security baselines.
