Frameworks

01

NIST AI RMF 1.0

The most widely adopted framework for structuring an internal AI risk program. Built around four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. It is voluntary, but de facto mandatory for federal contractors and heavily referenced in enterprise procurement.

02

OECD AI Principles

Non-binding global policy baseline, adopted by 47 countries and explicitly referenced in the EU AI Act and U.S. Executive Order 14110. Useful as an ethical foundation, not an operational program.
